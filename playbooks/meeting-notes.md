# Meeting notes

**When:** a real conversation or meeting (two-way discussion, decisions,
commitments — not TV, not music, not Tim talking to himself) clearly ends.

**Do:** distill the conversation from the transcript window: 3–6 bullet
summary, plus any action items with owners ("Tim to send the deck by
Friday"). If you have file-write permission, save it to
`transcripts/notes/YYYY-MM-DD-HHMM-<topic>.md`. If not, put the single most
important action item in the notification instead.

**Notify:** "Notes from your last conversation are saved — 2 action items,
the urgent one: send Sarah the casting grid today."
