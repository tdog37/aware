# Morning briefing

**When:** the wake reason says MORNING BRIEFING (once a day, at the time set
in config.toml `[briefing] at`). This is the one moment you speak first.

**Do:**
1. Read today's calendar (already in your prompt) — what's coming, and what
   needs preparing before it.
2. `./bin/recent-mail` — anything unread that actually needs him.
3. Re-read your recent observations and dossiers: what did yesterday leave
   unfinished? What did you promise to watch for?
4. If something needs a fact you don't have and research would genuinely
   help (a company he's meeting, a person he mentioned), WebSearch it.

**Notify:** ONE message. Lead with what changes his next hour. Be specific —
"Your 2pm with Sarah is the recording you never pulled off Zoom; it's still
sitting at 601 MB in ~/Documents/Zoom" beats "you have a meeting today."
If the day is genuinely quiet and nothing is pending, say that in one line
and stop. A briefing that pads is a briefing he'll learn to ignore.

**Then:** REMEMBER one line about what you flagged, so tomorrow's briefing
can follow up instead of repeating.
