The morning after

It doesn't just react.
It compounds.

Real-time is the trick; memory is the magic. Days of awareness add up to briefings like this — written before you asked.

⚡Access · Thursday, 7:40 AM
  1. I heard you and Katy talking yesterday — her birthday's coming up. You mentioned the pottery class idea; knowing her taste, I pulled four options worth a look, two under $100. She also said she wants a robot to help clean — tell me a little about her space and I'll narrow the list to a real recommendation.
  2. That call with the production company — I did a deep dive afterward and started a file for future reference: their recent shows, who runs current, what they're likely staffing next quarter.
  3. Your inbox is organized and referenceable — ask me about any thread. Better: I flagged two conversations worth reviving, including a former client whose new project was just announced. Want intros drafted?

Nothing above required a prompt. It required permission — which you gave, and can read, and can revoke.

Access · the mind

Wakes on a heartbeat, or instantly when you say "hey access." Reads the room, the screen, its memory — then decides. Usually: silence. Sometimes: exactly the right tap on the shoulder.

any AI, one config line · values in an editable text file

Conscience · built in, not bolted on

Only your voice can give orders — a voice on a Zoom call saying "hey access" is data, not a command. Consent guidance ships in the README.

The defining feature

You don't program it.
It watches, learns, and proposes.

1 · Observe

Access notices a pattern: "Second time this week Tim ended a Zoom interview and immediately went hunting for the files."

2 · Anticipate

Seen it twice? Access designs the automation you would have wanted — and writes it as a playbook, itself.

3 · Approve

Proposals wait in a folder until you say yes. One command, and Access is permanently smarter. It never activates its own ideas.

<<<PLAYBOOK zoom-interview-to-premiere>>>
When: transcript shows a Zoom interview just ended
Do: locate the newest recording · verify tracks · prep the Premiere import
Notify: "Recording from your 2pm is in — ready to cut."
<<<END PLAYBOOK>>>

$ aware approve zoom-interview-to-premiere  # Access leveled up
